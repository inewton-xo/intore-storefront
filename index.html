Terms & Conditions

  1. Orders. An order is confirmed only after we verify your payment and reply to you by WhatsApp or email.
  2. Prices. All prices are in Rwandan francs (RWF). We may correct pricing errors and will contact you before processing an affected order.
  3. Payment. Delivery orders are paid in advance, by card through our secure payment link or by mobile money (MoMo) to the number shown at checkout, before we dispatch. Pickup orders can be paid at pickup, in cash or MoMo, after you have inspected the item, or in advance if you prefer. Use your name or order details as the payment reference.
  4. Pickup and delivery. Pickup orders are held for you for 48 hours after we confirm them, then released if not collected. We confirm the pickup point and time with you on WhatsApp. Pay at pickup is not available for delivery orders. We deliver to the address you provide, and delivery times and any delivery fee are agreed with you when we confirm your order.
  5. Authenticity Guarantee. We guarantee that every item is genuine and matches its description, including condition and specifications. If you believe an item is not genuine or not as described, contact us within 7 days of receiving it and keep the item and its packaging unused where possible. If we confirm, or cannot show, that the item is genuine and as described, we refund you in full, including any delivery fee. If you pay at pickup, you may refuse an item that does not match its description without paying.
  6. Returns & refunds. Report damaged or wrong items within 48 hours of receiving them, with clear photos. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, normally within 5 working days.
  7. Availability. Items are subject to stock. If an item is unavailable we will offer a replacement or a full refund.
  8. Your data. We use your name, phone and address only to process and deliver your order, and we do not sell your information.
  9. Changes. We may update these terms at any time. Placing an order means you accept the terms shown on this page.
